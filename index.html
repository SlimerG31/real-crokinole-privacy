Real Crokinole

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026
Summary: Real Crokinole is an offline, standalone tabletop simulation game. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, advertising IDs, or usage analytics.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Real Crokinole ("we", "our", or "the app") treats user information. We value your privacy and believe you should be able to enjoy tabletop games without being tracked or having your personal information harvested.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). When you download, install, and play Real Crokinole:

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, address, or contact details.
  • We do not collect device hardware IDs, IMEI, or advertising identifiers (AAID / IDFA).
  • We do not collect location or GPS data.
  • We do not track browsing activity, in-app usage habits, or personal profiles.
  • We do not require user registration, logins, or social account linking.

3. Local Device Storage

All game settings (such as audio volume, board preferences, and game score history) are saved strictly locally on your device using standard mobile local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the application or clear app storage.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Real Crokinole contains no third-party analytics SDKs, user tracking platforms, or advertising networks. We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information to third parties.

5. Permissions

The application does not request sensitive Android runtime permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Location, or Storage access). Standard network permissions are present solely for web runtime compatibility and are not used to transmit personal data.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Real Crokinole is family-friendly and intended for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union) in accordance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update our Privacy Policy (for example, if future versions introduce optional cloud saves or online multiplayer), any revisions will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
